An Operations starter pack, for teams that answer "What does your team do?" with Operations.

It brings five short pages to fill in (How we work, Vendors, Processes, Tools we use, Glossary), each a heading, a line on what belongs there, a few fields and a request to hand your agent, plus a first-page prompt that asks the agent to fill in How we work with you. Its `operations-starter` plugin carries Anthropic's operations skills from knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0): vendor reviews, process docs, runbooks, change requests, capacity plans, risk assessments, compliance tracking, process optimization and status reports. Anthropic's connector list is left out, since each connector needs its own account. `SOURCE.md` in the plugin names the commit and what changed.
