The page title bar now lines up with the sidebar header row beside it. The two rows sit side by side under the toolbar and each used to arrive at its own height by accident: the sidebar frame's column padding plus whatever its header slot happened to be tall, against a page column's top padding plus an `<h1>`'s line box. On the Library the two answers were about 20px apart, and the seam between them is the first thing anyone sees below the toolbar.

Both rows are now one band: `--spacing-header` in the design tokens, spent by a single `HEADER_BAND` class in `shared/theme/header.ts`, with the contents centred in it so a 13px nav row and a 26px title share a line. The column offset above the band is shared too — the Library used to open on 34px where Knowledge opened on 12px, and that difference was the other half of the seam. Every page title bar goes through the band: the file page, the skill page, the tool page, the plugin, personal and locked plugin pages, and the Library's own galleries. None of them keeps a height, a top margin or a vertical padding of its own, and the per-page nudges that used to stand in for one (`mt-1.5` on two plugin headings) are gone.

The band is also the FIRST row of every page that has one. A plugin page's breadcrumb and a skill or tool page's back link used to sit in a row of their own above the title bar, which pushed the title bar down off the sidebar's line however exactly the two heights agreed. They ride on the band now, as its leading item. The breadcrumb stops short of the current page while it is there — the `<h1>` standing beside it is the current page, and a crumb repeating the heading it sits next to says the name twice on one line — and the locked plugin view's hand-copied trail is retired in favour of the one every other plugin page uses.

Three details moved to make a title bar one row rather than as tall as its contents. Page titles truncate instead of wrapping, and carry the full name in a `title`. The count under a gallery heading, the folder path under a plugin name and a tool's description now read below the band instead of inside it, which is where they already looked like they were. And the sidebar's header row is rendered whether or not anything fills it, so finishing onboarding — which retires the connect-your-agent pill — no longer pulls the nav up out of line with the page. A band that cannot wrap has to give something up when a narrow window runs it out of room, so every title bar now says what: the title, and then its trail or its chips, are clipped inside one group that takes the shortfall, while the controls beside them keep their width and stay clickable. The file page gives up title text before a reachable Share button; a plugin page truncates its name and then its breadcrumb rather than laying either across the manifest and action buttons.

Two tests hold it. One reads the sources: the token is declared once, `HEADER_BAND` is what spends it, every header row comes through it, no row sets a height, a top margin or a vertical padding of its own, and no column sets a top offset of its own — with the enumerations of rows and columns checked against a walk of the source tree, so a page added later cannot quietly keep a private header height. The other measures, against the PRODUCTION stylesheet: `src/index.css` is handed to Tailwind's own compiler and the CSS it emits is what the document is measured with, so the sidebar's header row and the page's title bar render to the same height at 1280, 1440 and 1920px — while the sidebar is collapsed and reopened, across a resize, and with an empty header slot — and a token renamed, moved out of `@theme` or compiled differently fails the suite.
