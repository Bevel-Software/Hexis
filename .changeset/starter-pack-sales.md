A Sales starter pack, for teams that answer "What does your team do?" with Sales.

It brings six short pages to fill in (About us, Customers, Pricing, Objections, How we sell, Glossary), each a heading, a line on what belongs there, a few fields and a request to hand your agent, plus a first-page prompt that asks the agent to fill in Customers with you. Its `sales-starter` plugin carries Anthropic's sales skills from knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0): call prep and summaries, account research, deal reviews, pipeline, forecast and 30 more. The skills look up the facts they need (your ideal customer, qualification framework, routing rules) in the knowledge base and offer to save what you tell them there. Anthropic's connector list is left out, since each connector needs its own account: the skills use whatever tools are connected, or files you upload. `SOURCE.md` in the plugin names the commit and what changed.
