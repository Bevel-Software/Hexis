The Skills & Tools sidebar switches between its two views with an IdP Groups / Advanced toggle under Everything and Owned by me, instead of stacking both under headings. IdP Groups lists your own space and the groups from the access rules; Advanced holds the `Skills/` and `Plugins/` folders as they are on disk, with no heading over them. The view you pick is remembered in your browser. A new plugin is started from the sidebar's right-click menu or from the Everything page.
