The Skills & Tools sidebar switches between its two views with a Teams / Advanced toggle under Everything and Owned by me, instead of stacking both under headings. Teams lists your own space and the groups from the access rules; Advanced holds the `Skills/` and `Plugins/` folders as they are on disk, with the New plugin button. The view you pick is remembered in your browser.
