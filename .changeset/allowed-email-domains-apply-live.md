The allowed email domains on the Single sign-on card apply without a restart, like the provider settings beside them: an SSO sign-in governed by the list reads the list in effect (the `ALLOWED_EMAIL_DOMAINS` environment variable, else the saved value), so a save applies to the next sign-in on the replica that served it and no longer answers `restartRequired`. The list governs existing accounts as much as new ones, as it did after a restart: narrowing it refuses an account outside it at its next SSO sign-in (and an Atlassian panel viewer at their next token), widening or clearing it admits them, and sessions already signed in are not ended. When the environment variable is not set, erasing the field clears the saved list, as its help text says, and everyone the provider authenticates can sign in again. A value that names no domain, or a token that is not a domain, is refused on save rather than saved as a list the check would read as no guard. Other replicas keep the list they read at boot until they restart, as they do for the provider settings.
