A general starter pack, for teams that answer "What does your team do?" with Something else.

It brings three short pages to fill in (About us, How we work, Glossary), each a heading, a line on what belongs there, a few fields and a request to hand your agent, plus a first-page prompt that asks the agent to fill in About us with you. Its `general-starter` plugin carries Anthropic's productivity skills from knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0), reworked so workplace memory lives in the knowledge base: the agent keeps a personal task list and a small working-memory file, and puts the team's shorthand, people and projects in the Glossary, People and Projects pages, work facts only and with your go-ahead, so every teammate's agent understands them. Anthropic's connector list and its local dashboard are left out. `SOURCE.md` in the plugin names the commit and what changed.
