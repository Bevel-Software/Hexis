A Support starter pack, for teams that answer "What does your team do?" with Support.

It brings five short pages to fill in (Products, Known issues, Tone of voice, Escalations, Glossary), each a heading, a line on what belongs there, a few fields and a request to hand your agent, plus a first-page prompt that asks the agent to fill in Products with you. Its `support-starter` plugin carries Anthropic's customer support skills from knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0): ticket triage, response drafting, escalations, customer research and knowledge base articles, reading the Hexis knowledge base wherever they look for documentation. Anthropic's connector list is left out, since each connector needs its own account. `SOURCE.md` in the plugin names the commit and what changed.
