The access verbs now say what they mean where a person picks or reads them. In Manage access, each item of the verb picker beside the add field and of a grantee row's picker, the verb summary on a row someone cannot change, and the Can edit and Owner request radios carry one line as their tooltip and accessible description; so does the Library's Owner pill on cards, plugin rows and the skill page. "Can read: open it and read it." "Can edit: everything Can read allows, plus changing it, approving change requests to it and choosing who has access." "Can download: everything Can read allows, plus downloading a copy, which Can edit does not include." "Owner: everything Can edit allows, plus downloading a copy. Owners are also named as the people to ask about it." On a row's picker an item's own note ("from Sales", "restricted here") comes first in its description, then the line. Who may do what is unchanged.
