"Connect as" appears only when there is a choice to make. With no agent on offer — every core-only deployment, and any distribution whose resolver lists none for the person — the OAuth consent step and the connection-key form show no "Connect as" group at all: the connection is Everything's, as every connection was before agents existed, instead of a radio group with one option. The group still appears when the agents could not be listed, since then they may well exist and the person confirms Everything knowingly. Likewise a key or connection made as Everything carries no "As Everything" tag on the External agent access page or in the Audit log; only one made as an agent says so.
