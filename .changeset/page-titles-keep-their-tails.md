Page titles no longer lose the bottoms of their letters.

A page title is one line at the display size, cut short with an ellipsis when it is too long. Its line height is tighter than the letters it sets, so the clipping that makes the ellipsis also cut off the tails of g, y and p: "Everything" at the top of Skills & Tools lost the bottoms of its g and y, and so did plugin, skill and knowledge page titles. The titles now have room for those tails without moving anything on the page.
