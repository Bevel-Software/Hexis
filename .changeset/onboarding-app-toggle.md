The toolbar shows Knowledge and Skills & Tools side by side instead of behind a menu.

- Beside the "Bevel" brand, a segmented switch lists every app, with the one you are in lifted onto the surface and marked as the current page. Each segment is a link to its app; clicking the app you are already in does nothing, as choosing it in the menu did.
- The menu ("Bevel / Knowledge ▾") remains for a deployment with more than three apps, and on a compact toolbar where the switch would not fit.
